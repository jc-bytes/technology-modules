One sound, many measurements

Your voice makes tiny movements in the air. A microphone turns them into an electrical signal. A computer measures this signal.

A sample is one measurement at one moment. The computer stores a number for each sample. Many measurements can describe the same sound.

Sample rate means how many measurements the computer takes each second. Each dot below stands for one measurement, not one sound.
