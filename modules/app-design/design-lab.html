Why do these words stand out?

Contrast means how different light and dark colours look. Dark words on a pale background are easier to see. White words stand out on a dark button.

04 / Check and explain

Try your real app.

Open your real app. Ask a partner to choose a meal and press Confirm.

If something is hard to use, fix that thing. Try again.

The words are too small
  1. In Designer, click the part with small words.
  2. Find FontSize. Try 18.
  3. Read it on your real device again.
The whole message does not fit
  1. Click ResultLabel in Designer.
  2. Find Width. Choose Fill parent. This lets it use the screen width.
  3. Check the whole message in your real app again.
