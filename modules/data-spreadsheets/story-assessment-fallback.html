Planning the school club afternoon

Maya is helping her teacher prepare a guide for the school club afternoon. Students want to know which clubs they can visit and how long each activity lasts. Maya reads the teachers’ notes before making a table.

The Art club will start with a drawing activity that lasts 12 minutes. Next, the Chess club will play a short game for 18 minutes. In the Music club, students will practise a song for 24 minutes. The Drama club will rehearse a scene for 30 minutes.

The Dance club plans a 36-minute practice. After that, the Sport club will run a team activity for 42 minutes. The Science club needs 48 minutes for an experiment. Finally, the Robotics club will spend 54 minutes building and testing a small robot.

Maya needs a clear table so students can compare the clubs and plan their afternoon.
