Read the table title

A table keeps related facts in rows and columns.

The title tells you what the whole table is about. Here, it tells us that the numbers show time spent in school clubs.

Read down a column

A column runs down the table. Its name at the top is called a header.

Read across a row

A row runs across the table. Read the highlighted row: A01, Art, 35.

A01 is a student ID. This student spends 35 minutes in Art club. The whole row is one record: the facts about that student kept together.
