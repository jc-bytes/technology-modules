Column: A line of cells going down a table.

A cell address

A cell is one box in a spreadsheet. Its address has a column letter first and a row number second. B2 means column B, row 2. A row runs across. A column runs down.

Each number in this table counts students attending the activity on that day.

A table keeps facts together

Each row describes one club. Each column holds one type of fact. Keep the club name and its minutes on the same row.

Watch the model

The Art club meets for 20 minutes. The Chess club meets for 30 minutes. On Model, the headings are Club and Minutes. Art goes in A2 and 20 in B2. Chess goes in A3 and 30 in B3. Type numbers without the word minutes.

ClubMinutes
Art20
Chess30

Complete two together

Click Guided. Music meets for 15 minutes. Drama meets for 25 minutes. Enter the two club names in A2:A3 and the numbers in B2:B3. Read each row back as a sentence.

Enter four yourself

Click Practice. Dance meets for 20 minutes. Sport meets for 40 minutes. Science meets for 30 minutes. Robotics meets for 50 minutes. Enter the records in that order in A2:B5.

Select A1:B1 and click Bold. Double-click the right edge of column A at the top to fit the names. Do the same for B. Compare all eight entries with the story. In A8 write one sentence explaining the Robotics row.

Check and save

Build a column chart

Ungraded class practice. Use this page instead of the matching explanation in your activity. Record answers in the assigned Word activity or real software; no extra module report is required.

A column shows a number

A column chart uses one bar for each club. The taller the bar, the more minutes the club meets. The bottom axis names the clubs. The side axis counts minutes.

Watch the model

On Model, select A1:B3. Choose Insert, Column, 2-D Clustered Column. Set the chart title to Club minutes. Choose Chart Design, Add Chart Element, Axis Titles. Name the horizontal axis Club and the vertical axis Minutes. The Art bar reaches 20 and Chess reaches 30.

Build one together

On Guided, select A1:B3. Repeat those steps. Check that Music reaches 15 and Drama reaches 25.

Build your own

On Practice, select A1:B5. Make a 2-D Clustered Column chart. Set its title to Club minutes and axis titles to Club and Minutes. Move the chart to the right of column C. Check all four bars against your table. Move your Robotics sentence from A8 to A18, below the chart, and check that the chart agrees.

Check your chart

There are four bars, one for each club. The Minutes axis starts at 0. If necessary, right-click its numbers, choose Format Axis and set Minimum to 0. Your sentence names the club, its minutes and what they mean.
