Two words

Row: A line of cells going across a table.

Column: A line of cells going down a table.

A cell address

A cell is one box in a spreadsheet. Its address has a column letter first and a row number second. B2 means column B, row 2. A row runs across. A column runs down.

Each number in this table counts students attending the activity on that day.

2Art69

Worked example

Find B2: start at column B and move down to row 2. The value is 6. It tells us that 6 students attend Art on Tuesday.

Turn sentences into a table

Offline practice: use paper when the module is unavailable. Keep each club and its minutes on one row. Use the headings Club and Minutes.

Model: Art meets for 20 minutes. Chess meets for 30 minutes. Write a note explaining how the facts become two columns.

Together: Music meets for 15 minutes. Drama meets for 25 minutes. Draw a two-row table.

On your own: Dance meets for 20 minutes. Sport meets for 40 minutes. Science meets for 30 minutes. Robotics meets for 50 minutes. Draw four records in that order, make the headings stand out, and leave enough room for each word. Compare all eight entries with the sentences.

Write one sentence explaining the Robotics row. Give your paper to your teacher. Enter and check your work in the module when access returns.

Build a column chart

Offline practice: use your completed Class 2 table. If it is unavailable, finish the Class 2 paper activity first.

A column height shows minutes. The bottom axis names the clubs. The side axis counts minutes and starts at zero. Use the Art 20 and Chess 30 example to write a note about how a number becomes a column.

Together: sketch a chart for Music 15 and Drama 25. Label it Club minutes, with Club along the bottom and Minutes along the side.

On your own: sketch one column for each record in your Class 2 table. Label both axes, start at zero, and check every height. Explain what the Robotics column means. Give your paper to your teacher and complete the interactive chart when access returns.

Watch the model

Art meets for 10 minutes. Chess meets for 20 minutes. Model shows both records and their chart. The Art bar reaches 10. One row becomes one bar.

ClubMinutes
Art10
Chess20

Try together

Music meets for 30 minutes. Drama meets for 40 minutes. On Guided, enter the two records in A2:B3. Select A1:B3 and choose Insert, Column, 2-D Clustered Column. Set the chart title to Club minutes. Under Chart Design, Add Chart Element, Axis Titles, enter Club horizontally and Minutes vertically.

Make your own

Dance meets for 15 minutes. Sport meets for 25 minutes. Science meets for 35 minutes. Robotics meets for 45 minutes. On Practice, type Club in A1 and Minutes in B1. Enter these four records in order in A2:B5. Bold the two headings and double-click each column edge to fit its text.

Select A1:B5. Make a 2-D Clustered Column chart titled Club minutes with axes Club and Minutes. Move it to the right of column C. Check that the vertical axis starts at 0; use Format Axis, Minimum 0 if needed. Compare each bar with the story.

In A18 write one sentence explaining the Science value. Include the club, number and minutes. Name its cell address B4 in the sentence.
