6A

Measure a rectangular eraser

Use a ruler marked in millimetres. Put the eraser edge at 0. Read the mark at the other edge. Measure the long edge, short edge and height. Write mm after each value. Ten millimetres make one centimetre.

Watch, then try together

The example eraser is 60 mm long, 20 mm wide and 10 mm high. Together, check its long edge from 0 to 60. Starting at the plastic edge instead of the 0 mark changes the reading.

Measure yours

Measure your eraser twice. Record your own values, not the example values.

DimensionFirst reading mmSecond reading mmChecked value mm
Length________________________
Width________________________
Height________________________

If the readings differ, line up 0 again and repeat. My checked eraser is ___ mm long, ___ mm wide and ___ mm high.

Build one box in Tinkercad

Open Tinkercad from Google Classroom and sign in with your school account. Choose Create, 3D Design. Rename the design G6_Eraser_Firstname_Lastname. Work in millimetres.

Drag one solid Box from Basic Shapes onto the workplane. Select it. Click a corner sizing handle to show the two base dimensions. Click each dimension number, type the checked length or width from your record, and press Enter. Click the top white sizing handle and type your checked height. Keep the box on the workplane.

Watch and try together
