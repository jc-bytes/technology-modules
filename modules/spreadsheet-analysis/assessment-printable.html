Grade 7 assessment. Printable class fallback. Write answers on paper and complete the spreadsheet in Google Sheets.

Build and explain a spreadsheet

Work independently. Build your spreadsheet in Google Sheets and answer the questions on the assessment website. Your teacher marks both files.

Build your table

Create a new Google Sheets spreadsheet named Recycling followed by your name. Name the sheet Recycling. Recreate the recycling table below in A1:B9. Put Team in A1 and Bottles in B1. Keep the team order shown and enter the counts as numbers. Make both headers bold and widen the columns so all text is visible.

TeamBottles
Red14
Blue22
Green18
Yellow26
Orange30
Purple16
White24
Black10

Calculate

In A12 write Total bottles. In A13 write Average per team. In A14 write Largest count. Enter formulas in B12, B13 and B14 to calculate these three results using all eight bottle counts. Choose the functions and cell range yourself. Keep working formulas in the cells for your teacher to inspect.

Answer on the website

1. What value is in B4?

2. What cell address contains Purple?

3. Which team is in row 6?

4. What does your total tell you about the bottles collected? Include the result in a complete English sentence.

5. What does your average tell you about the bottles collected per team? Include the result in a complete English sentence.

6. Which team collected the most bottles, and how many did it collect? Write a complete English sentence.

Submit

Download your spreadsheet from Google Sheets as Microsoft Excel (.xlsx). Download your answers from the website. Save both files and submit them to this assessment in Google Classroom.
