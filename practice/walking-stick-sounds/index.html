Connection help

Use Chrome on a laptop or supported Android device. Turn on Bluetooth, then choose your BBC micro:bit after clicking Connect.

Your micro:bit needs the Bluetooth UART program and connection setting from the walking-stick guide. Reconnect after uploading a new program.

Chrome on iPhone or iPad does not offer this connection. School device settings may also block Bluetooth.

The updated program sends a result and distance, for example NEAR,20.3. The distance is an ultrasonic estimate rounded to 0.1 cm. UNKNOWN means no usable distance. Older status-only programs show no number. NEAR plays the warning. FAR and UNKNOWN stop it. If messages stop arriving, the page pauses the warning.
