Ask four privacy questions

Some data helps an app do its job. Other data may be used for ads.

1. What data? Name the information.

2. Why? Find what it is used for.

3. Who? Find who can use or see it.

4. What choice? Look for a setting or permission.

Use the notice as evidence. A private profile does not mean the app collects nothing.

Read a short notice

SketchSpace is a fictional drawing app. This is a short practice notice.

Drawings: saved on your device so you can reopen them.

Drawing-tool activity: sent to an ad partner to choose targeted ads. An ad partner is another company that helps choose ads.

Your choice: personalized ads are optional. With the switch off, drawing still works and future tool activity is not sent for these ads.

The switch does not delete data already shared. Settings can limit sharing; they do not guarantee zero data use.

Follow a permission decision

SketchSpace asks for the camera when Alex chooses to add a photo.

What data? A new photo.

Why? To add that photo to the drawing.

Who? Alex uses the photo in the app. This notice does not say it is posted publicly.

Choice: allow the camera only while taking the photo. Alex can draw without adding a photo.

This permission is for one feature. It is not permission for every possible use.
