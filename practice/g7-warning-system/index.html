The greenhouse alarm

Help the greenhouse keeper fix the radio link. One micro:bit stays by the plant. The other hangs by the door. The buttons stand in for a distance sensor measuring how far the plant has been lifted.

Try the fault. On a fresh starter, press B on the plant board. That means 19 cm. Look at both boards. Which one reacts?

Change the block inside if distance > 18. The door board knows the radio words ALERT and CLEAR. Which message should it get at 19 cm?

Test your edit. Wait for both boards to start, then press B on the sender. Look for a skull on the door board and listen for its short beep.

The alarm belongs on only above 18 cm. Complete the else branch so the door can turn it off.

Test the change. Press B to turn the warning on. Then press the sender’s A+B button for 18 cm. Watch what happens at the door.

Set the receiver’s beep to 200 milliseconds. Click the face of the door board to enable sound, then press B on the plant board.

Change the duration to 500 milliseconds. Keep the pitch at 440 Hz. Test with B at the same volume.
