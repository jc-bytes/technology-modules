Notice what the app does

Some app features encourage us to keep watching or return to the app.

FeatureWhat happens
AutoplayThe next video starts by itself.
Endless scrollingMore posts appear as you scroll.
NotificationsAn alert asks you to open the app.

Example: Sam plans to watch one video. Autoplay starts another. Turning autoplay off lets Sam choose when to continue.

A feature can be useful. We can still choose settings that help us control our attention.
