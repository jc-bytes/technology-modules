Privacy includes what apps collect

Privacy means having control over your personal information.

You share information when you post. Apps can also collect information as you use them.

Example: a shop records which shoes Sam views. It uses that information to choose a shoe ad for Sam.

Source: Common Sense Education

Check what an app needs

A permission lets an app use something on your device.

Sam uses a drawing app. The app asks for a phone contact list.

Worked example: drawing does not need phone contacts. Sam chooses Don’t allow.

Before sharing, ask: What information? Who can see it? Does the app need it?
