Make the word stand out

In App Inventor · Designer

WordLabel is the text Ju. Changing its font makes the same word easier to read; it does not change the recording.

Select WordLabel in Components. In Properties, set FontSize to 40, check FontBold, and set TextAlignment to center.

Check: Ju becomes large, bold and centered.

Style the meaning

In App Inventor · Designer

In App Inventor · Designer
