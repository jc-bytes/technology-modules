Staying on one page and scrolling down is vertical reading. Its own About page is a starting point, not independent confirmation.

Search engines help locate sources. A result’s position does not prove it is reliable. Reference pages can lead you to cited sources; inspect those sources.

Explain lateral reading

Vertical reading stays on one page. Lateral reading checks other pages for information about the claim and its source.
